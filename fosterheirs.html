About Fosterheirs

Where faith meets science, whole people are formed.

Founded in 2023 within the Heirs Specialist Hospital complex in Oye-Ekiti, Fosterheirs brings together medical practitioners, licensed therapists and faith-integrated counsellors. It is a sanctuary for holistic healing, addressing addiction, trauma, marital crises and emotional instability.

Our foundational belief is simple: lasting healing must address the mind, the body and the soul together. Or, as our founder puts it - "God designed the mind just as He designed the soul."
