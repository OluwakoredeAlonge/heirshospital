Quick Answers

Before you visit

A few things patients ask most often.

All FAQs
Do I need a referral to see a specialist?
You can book a consultation directly by phone, WhatsApp or the form above. If you have a referral letter or previous results, bring them along; they help our doctors understand your history.
Do you accept NHIS and HMO insurance?
Yes. Heirs is an NHIS-accredited provider and works with several HMOs. Bring your card and a valid ID; our billing desk will confirm coverage before treatment.
How quickly can I get an appointment?
Send your request and our front desk will offer you the earliest available slot with the right doctor when we confirm.
What if it's an emergency?
Call +234 803 638 6440 immediately or come straight to the Oye-Ekiti main branch. Our emergency room and ICU are open 24 hours a day, and ambulance transport can be dispatched.
