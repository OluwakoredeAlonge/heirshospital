About the hospital

What is Heirs Multispecialist Hospital?
Heirs Multispecialist Hospital (also known as Heirs Specialist Hospital) is a 50-bed, NHIS-accredited specialist hospital in Ekiti State, founded by Dr. Michael Soje and Dr. (Mrs) Anthonia Soje. It offers renal dialysis, CT scan, intensive care, surgery, cardiology, obstetrics, dental, eye care, laboratory & blood bank, pharmacy, mental-health services and 24/7 emergency care from two branches in Oye-Ekiti and Ado-Ekiti.
What are your opening hours?
Monday–Friday 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM; Saturday 9:00 AM – 8:30 PM; Sunday 1:00 PM – 8:00 PM. Our emergency room and ICU at the Oye-Ekiti main branch are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
Where are you located?
Oye-Ekiti (Main Branch): Beside Aluko House, Irare Estate, Oye-Ekiti, Ekiti State.
Ado-Ekiti Branch: Plot 3, Orimolade Crescent, Ilokun Estate, Ado-Ekiti, Ekiti State. Both are in serene, secure neighbourhoods with parking. See directions.
Who are the doctors at Heirs?
Our clinical team is led by Medical Director Dr. Michael Soje (internal medicine & gastroenterology) and includes resident physicians, nurses and allied-health professionals, complemented by visiting specialist consultants in nephrology, cardiology, surgery, obstetrics & gynaecology, paediatrics and more. Mental-health services are led by Dr. Anthonia Soje through Fosterheirs.
Are you NHIS accredited?
Yes. Heirs is an accredited provider under the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS/NHIA) and also partners with private HMOs. See the payments section for details.

Appointments

How do I book an appointment?
Use the online booking form, call +234 803 638 6440, WhatsApp +234 704 248 1085, or walk into either branch. Booked patients are seen first.
How far in advance should I book?
General consultations are usually available same-day or next-day. For visiting specialists (e.g. cardiology, orthopaedics) we recommend booking 3–7 days ahead so we can place you in the next clinic day.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes, free of charge, up to 24 hours before your appointment. Just call or WhatsApp us. Late cancellations may attract a small fee for specialist clinics.
Do I need a referral to see a specialist?
No referral is required. If you have one, bring it, along with previous results, so the specialist has your full history.
What should I bring to my appointment?
A valid ID, your NHIS/HMO card (if applicable), previous medical records or test results, and a list of your current medications and allergies. Arrive 15 minutes early for registration.

Payments & insurance

What payment methods do you accept?
Cash, bank transfer, debit/credit cards (POS), NHIS and partner HMO coverage. Receipts and itemised invoices are issued for every payment.
Do you accept health insurance / HMOs?
Yes. We are NHIS-accredited and work with several HMOs. Bring your card and ID; our billing desk verifies coverage before treatment and explains any co-payments up front.
Can I get an invoice or medical report for my employer or insurer?
Yes. Medical reports, sick-leave certificates, fitness certificates and itemised invoices can be requested at the front desk or by email. Reports are usually ready within 2 working days.
Do you offer payment plans for expensive treatments?
For major procedures such as surgery, dialysis programmes or joint replacement, our billing team can discuss staged payment arrangements. Ask to speak to the administrator before your procedure.

Emergencies

Is your emergency department open 24/7?
Yes. The emergency room and intensive care unit at the Oye-Ekiti main branch are staffed 24 hours a day, backed by 24-hour solar power. Call +234 803 638 6440.
Do you have ambulance services?
Yes. We operate ambulance services with trained paramedics for emergency pick-up and inter-facility transfers within and around Ekiti State.
What qualifies as a medical emergency?
Chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), severe bleeding, serious injury or burns, loss of consciousness, seizures, severe abdominal pain, complications in pregnancy, or a very high fever in a child. If in doubt, call us.
Do you have an intensive care unit?
Yes. Our ICU provides continuous monitoring, ventilator support and advanced life support for critically ill patients, including post-operative care.

Services & results

What medical services do you offer?
Clinical consultations, renal dialysis (haemo & peritoneal), heart & cardiovascular treatment with cardiopulmonary tests, CT scan, digital X-ray & ultrasound, general & specialised surgery, hip & knee replacement, obstetrics & gynaecology, cancer treatment (prostate, breast etc.), intensive & critical care, full laboratory with blood bank, complete dental services, eye treatment, pharmacy, nutrition & dietetics, physiotherapy, and trauma & addiction-recovery therapy. See all services.
Do you offer dialysis?
Yes. Heirs runs a renal dialysis unit offering haemodialysis and a peritoneal dialysis option, one of the few centres in Ekiti to do so. Book a nephrology consultation to be assessed and scheduled.
Do you have a CT scanner?
Yes. Our radiology department offers computerised tomography (CT) scans alongside digital X-ray and ultrasound, with reports usually available within 24 hours.
How long do lab results take?
Routine tests (full blood count, malaria, blood sugar, urinalysis) are typically ready the same day. Chemistry and specialised tests take 24–48 hours; cultures take 3–5 days. Results can be collected in person or sent securely by email/WhatsApp on request.
Do you perform surgeries?
Yes, general and specialised surgery including hip & knee replacement, cancer surgery and obstetric & gynaecological procedures, with post-operative ICU care available.
Can I get medical certificates or reports?
Yes. Sick-leave certificates, fitness-to-work reports, pre-employment medicals and detailed medical reports are available on request from the front desk.

Our programmes

What is Fosterheirs Mental Health Consultancy?
Fosterheirs is the hospital's mental-health arm, founded in 2023 by Dr. Anthonia Soje. It offers psycho-trauma therapy, outpatient and residential addiction recovery, marriage & premarital counselling, individual therapy, self-help courses and professional certifications. Learn more.
What is the Methuselah Project and who is eligible?
A free monthly healthcare clinic for elders aged 65 and above who face financial hardship, run by Heirs Missions every third Saturday (8 AM – 2 PM) at the Oye-Ekiti branch. Consultations, lab tests, medications and health education are 100% free. Pre-registration is required at least 3 days ahead. Register an elder.
What does Heirs Institute of Allied Health offer?
Accredited programmes for Community Health Extension Workers, Pharmacy Technicians, Medical Laboratory Technicians, Health Information Management, Radiography Technicians, Dental Technology, Physiotherapy Assistants and Environmental Health Technology, with clinical practice inside the hospital and scholarships available. See programmes.
Can I do a hospital attachment or internship at Heirs?
Yes. We host clinical attachments for nursing, laboratory, pharmacy and allied-health students, subject to capacity. Email contact@heirsspecialisthospital.com.ng with your institution's letter.
