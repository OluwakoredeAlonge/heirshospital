Why Heirs Institute

Learn where the patients are.

Most health colleges teach in classrooms and send students elsewhere for practice. At Heirs, the hospital is the classroom, students rotate through our laboratory, pharmacy, radiology, wards and dialysis unit from their first term.

Admissions

Entry requirements

  • Minimum of SSCE / WAEC / NECO with five credits, including English Language and Mathematics
  • Science subjects (Biology, Chemistry, Physics) required for laboratory, pharmacy and radiography programmes
  • Minimum age of 16 years at the time of admission
  • Completed application form, academic credentials and birth certificate
  • Four passport photographs and a medical fitness certificate
