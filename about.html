Our Purpose

Excellence in healthcare, delivered with compassion.

Located in serene neighbourhoods in Oye-Ekiti and Ado-Ekiti, Heirs was built on a simple conviction: families in Ekiti deserve access to specialist care without travelling to Lagos, Abuja or abroad.

From renal dialysis and CT imaging to intensive care, surgery and integrated mental-health services, every unit is designed to bring the best of medical practice home, with the warmth of a family hospital.

Our Mission

To provide world-class, affordable and compassionate specialist healthcare to the people of Ekiti State and beyond, combining expert clinicians, modern technology and a patient-first culture.

To be the leading multispecialist hospital in South-West Nigeria, ending the need for medical tourism by delivering care comparable to the best hospitals in Nigeria and internationally.

Our Promise

Every patient is treated as family: listened to, respected and cared for holistically, mind, body and spirit, from the first consultation to full recovery.

Our Journey

A decade of growing with Ekiti.

What began as a specialist hospital in Oye-Ekiti has grown into a health ecosystem spanning two branches, a mental-health consultancy, an allied-health institute and a free monthly clinic for elders.

Oye-Ekiti Main BranchBeside Aluko House, Irare Estate
2015

Heirs Specialist Hospital opens in Oye-Ekiti

Dr. Michael Soje and Dr. (Mrs) Anthonia Soje establish Heirs beside Aluko House, Irare Estate, Oye-Ekiti, as a world-class specialist hospital set to end medical tourism in Nigeria.

Expansion

Second branch in Ado-Ekiti

A second branch opens at Plot 3, Orimolade Crescent, Ilokun Estate, Ado-Ekiti, bringing Heirs care to the state capital.

Growth

Multi-specialist & diagnostic services

Renal dialysis with a peritoneal option, CT scan, intensive & critical care, a full laboratory with blood bank, 24-hour solar power and ambulance services make Heirs a true multi-specialist hospital and diagnostic centre.

2023

Fosterheirs Mental Health Consultancy

Founded within the hospital complex to integrate medicine, psychology and faith for trauma therapy, addiction recovery and marriage restoration.

Training

Heirs Institute of Allied Health

A subsidiary of the hospital, established to address the critical shortage of skilled allied-health professionals in Nigeria, drawing on over a decade of healthcare experience in Ekiti State.

Outreach

The Methuselah Project

Heirs Missions, the charitable division of the hospital, runs a free monthly healthcare programme for elders aged 65 and above: consultations, tests and medications at no cost.

10 Years

10th anniversary celebration

Heirs marks a decade of care with the commissioning of a new multipurpose building at Oye-Ekiti and the launch of books by co-founder Dr. Anthonia Soje.

Today

50 beds. 16,612+ patients. One family.

Two branches, 16+ specialist services, NHIS accreditation and a team of specialist and visiting doctors, still guided by the same promise of care.

Our Founders

The physician couple behind Heirs

A husband-and-wife team who combined internal medicine and mental health into one integrated vision of care.

MS

Dr. Michael Soje

Co-Founder & Medical Director

A distinguished consultant physician in internal medicine with extensive experience and research interest in gastroenterology. Dr. Soje is affiliated with the Federal Teaching Hospital, Ido-Ekiti and Afe Babalola University (ABUAD), and his contributions to the understanding of gastroenterological conditions have advanced medical knowledge in Nigeria.

As Medical Director, he leads Heirs' clinical governance, from the dialysis unit and ICU to specialist clinics, ensuring every protocol meets international best practice.

AS

Dr. (Mrs) Anthonia Yemisi Soje

Co-Founder, Hospital Administrator & Lead Therapist

Physician, hospital administrator, entrepreneur, educator and speaker. Dr. Anthonia Soje is a board-certified psycho-trauma therapist, addiction-recovery therapist, certified transformational life coach and certified Christian marriage & sexuality coach. She founded Fosterheirs Mental Health Consultancy within the hospital complex in 2023.

She is the author of ten books, including The Addiction Compass, Echoes of Eden, Sanctuary and The Fourfold Path to Freedom, and practises CBT, Compassionate Inquiry, Inner-Child Work and Hypnotherapy.
