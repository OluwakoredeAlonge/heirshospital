The Methuselah Project

Free monthly healthcare for elders aged 65 and above.

An initiative of Heirs Missions, the charitable division of Heirs Multispecialist Hospital, providing completely free medical consultations, laboratory tests, medications and health education to financially disadvantaged seniors, delivered with dignity.

What Elders Receive

Care with dignity, at no cost.

Many of our elders live with hypertension, diabetes or arthritis that has never been properly diagnosed or treated, simply because care was unaffordable. The Methuselah Project removes that barrier entirely.

Eligibility

Who can register?

  • Aged 65 years or older
  • Facing financial hardship that makes regular care difficult
  • Holds a valid government-issued ID (National ID, voter's card, etc.)
  • Pre-registered at least 3 days before the clinic day (mandatory)

How to register

  1. 1Call 0704 248 1085, use the form, or visit the hospital in person.
  2. 2Provide the elder's name, age, contact details and main health concerns.
  3. 3Receive confirmation and arrive with a valid ID on clinic day (8 AM – 2 PM).
