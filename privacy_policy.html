1 Who we are

Heirs Multispecialist Hospital ("Heirs", "we", "us") operates hospitals at Beside Aluko House, Irare Estate, Oye-Ekiti and Plot 3, Orimolade Crescent, Ilokun Estate, Ado-Ekiti, Ekiti State, Nigeria, together with Fosterheirs Mental Health Consultancy, Heirs Institute of Allied Health and Heirs Missions (the Methuselah Project). We are the data controller for personal and health information collected through our facilities and this website, and we process it in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable medical-confidentiality rules.

2 Information we collect

  • Identity & contact details, name, date of birth, gender, phone, email, address, next of kin, ID number.
  • Health information, symptoms, diagnoses, test results, imaging, prescriptions, treatment notes, mental-health and addiction-recovery records, allergies and family history.
  • Insurance & billing - NHIS/HMO numbers, invoices and payment records (we never store full card numbers).
  • Website data, information you type into our appointment, feedback, admission or Fosterheirs enquiry forms, and basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited.
  • Programme data - Methuselah Project pre-registrations (age, financial-hardship confirmation, ID type) and Heirs Institute applications (academic credentials, photographs, medical-fitness certificates).

3 How we use your information

  • To provide safe, coordinated medical care across our departments and branches.
  • To schedule and confirm appointments, send results and follow-up reminders.
  • To process NHIS/HMO claims and billing.
  • To run our programmes, admissions, scholarships, therapy sessions and free elder clinics.
  • To improve our services through anonymised quality audits and patient-feedback surveys.
  • To meet legal and public-health reporting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use health information for marketing without your explicit consent.

4 Sharing & disclosure

Your information is shared only with: clinicians involved in your care (including visiting consultants); laboratories, imaging centres or hospitals we refer you to, with your knowledge; your NHIS/HMO for claims; regulators or courts where the law requires; and trusted service providers (e.g. secure messaging, hosting) bound by confidentiality. Mental-health and addiction-recovery records held by Fosterheirs are subject to additional confidentiality and are shared within the hospital only with your consent or in an emergency.

5 Security & retention

Records are protected by physical access controls, role-based access to electronic systems, staff confidentiality agreements and regular training. Medical records are retained for the periods required by Nigerian law and professional guidelines, after which they are securely destroyed. Website form submissions are used to respond to you and are not retained longer than necessary.

6 Your rights

  • Access a copy of your medical record and request a medical report.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Object to, or withdraw consent for, non-essential uses such as newsletters.
  • Request restriction or deletion where the law permits.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise these rights, contact our records office using the details in section 10. We respond within 30 days.

7 Cookies & website data

This website uses only essential technical storage to remember your preferences (for example, a collapsed menu). We do not use advertising trackers. Embedded services - Google Maps, Google Fonts and the Fosterheirs learning platform, may set their own cookies according to their policies. Links to Selar and WhatsApp open third-party services with their own terms.

8 Children & elders

Information about children is collected from a parent or guardian. For Methuselah Project participants, a family member may register on the elder's behalf; the elder's consent is confirmed on clinic day. We take extra care with the records of vulnerable patients.
