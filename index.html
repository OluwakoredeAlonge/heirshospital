About Heirs

Bringing the best of medical practice to Ekiti.

Founded by Dr. Michael Soje and Dr. (Mrs) Anthonia Soje, Heirs Multi-Specialist Hospital and Diagnostic Centre is a world-class specialist hospital set to end medical tourism in Nigeria, offering high-quality medical services comparable to what is obtainable in leading hospitals in Nigeria and elsewhere, from two serene neighbourhoods in Ekiti State.

  • Renal dialysis with peritoneal option, CT scan and full radiology suite
  • Intensive & critical care, 24/7 emergency and ambulance services
  • General & specialised surgery, obstetrics, oncology and joint replacement
  • Integrated mental-health, addiction recovery and counselling services

Our Founders

A physician couple with one mission: excellent care, close to home.

Heirs was founded by a husband-and-wife team of physicians who believed Ekiti deserved the same standard of care found in Lagos, Abuja or abroad, and built it.
